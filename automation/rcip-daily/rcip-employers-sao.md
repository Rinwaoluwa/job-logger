# RCIP designated employers (from @sao.immigration Instagram carousel)
Source date shown on slides: not shown (no publication or "as of" date on any slide; the intake notes refer to 2026, e.g. "Intake: closed for 2026", "No 2026 RCIP intakes left listed", "36 spots in 2026")

Notes:
- Slide 1 is the title slide ("14 COMMUNITIES. HUNDREDS OF DESIGNATED EMPLOYERS. HERE THEY ARE.").
- Slides 2, 3, 5, 6 are intro/explainer slides (the problem, what RCIP is, how hiring works, precautions incl. "Designated ≠ hiring.").
- Slide 4 lists the 14 communities: Ontario (North Bay & Area, Sudbury, Timmins, Sault Ste. Marie, Thunder Bay), Nova Scotia (Pictou County), Manitoba (Steinbach, Altona/Rhineland, Brandon), Saskatchewan (Moose Jaw), Alberta (Claresholm), British Columbia (West Kootenay, North Okanagan-Shuswap, Peace Liard). Note on slide: "Sudbury and Timmins also run FCIP, a separate French-language pilot."
- Slide 18 is a "SAVE THIS" eligibility summary (experience, language, education, funds, intent, forms). Slide 19 is the outro/call to action ("Comment STRATEGY").
- Employer lists are laid out in two columns; "slide order" below is left column top to bottom, then right column top to bottom.
- The lists are partial samples. Several slides link to a full list, and three say how many more are designated but "not currently hiring".
